What this is / what it trades Each finished branch is a short paragraph a human wrote to be read, with the verdict as a verb at the end of it. Numbers move to the last line, where they belong; nothing leads with a count. Trades: it is the longest of the four vertically, it is the only one that needs the session to write real prose per unit (a bad summariser makes this shape worse than a table), and it is the worst for finding one specific card fast.

How it held up in Chaos — does not survive intact This is the shape the skin fights, and it is worth saying plainly rather than papering over. B's entire hierarchy is one big soft sentence over smaller prose. Chaos's display face cannot set a sentence: at Calm's 23px, a 60-character lede is four lines of pixel type and the page stops being readable at a glance. Held to 11px — the product's own --size-h2 — it fits on one line, but it is now smaller than the 15px paragraph beneath it, which inverts the exact hierarchy the shape is built on.

The only honest fix was to rebuild the lede's authority out of something other than size: it becomes a name plate — header gradient, ink outline, bevel — sitting on top of its own paragraph, which is a real JRPG pattern and does read. But that is a construction change, not a costume: B is the only one of the four where Chaos alters what the thing is, and it spends that change adding furniture to the option that was already the longest vertically. If you want B's words in Chaos, the workable version is this page with the lede handed back to the body face and no pixel type above 11px anywhere — a legitimate thing to pick, it just means one surface where Chaos's display face never appears.
