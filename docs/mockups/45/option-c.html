When a packet has no screenshot, the tile is its first check step in the same space — the grid never shows an empty grey rectangle, and a change with no picture is never quietly ranked below one that has one.

how this shape handles non-UI work
What this is / what it trades The evidence is the object; the words are its caption. For UI work this is the fastest possible yes — you recognise a good screen in about a second, and the 6-card branch reads as one picture with a filmstrip rather than six near-identical rows. Trades: it flatly favours work that photographs well — a server fix with three checks and no picture is a text tile in a room full of images — it is the most vertical space per decision, and a screenshot can look right while the thing behind it is wrong, so the check steps get demoted to a click.

In Chaos Tiles get the ink outline and hard bevel and become inventory slots; the badge is a gold plate in the corner; the bracket around a unit is the party frame. Because the pictures carry the meaning and Chaos only restyles frames, this is the shape that survives the skin change with the least translation work.
