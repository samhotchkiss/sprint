What this is / what it trades One decision fills the screen, at the size of something you are meant to read; the rest of the queue is four quiet lines with no buttons on them. Trades: you lose the overview — you cannot see all four packets at once, cherry-pick, or compare two branches, and a queue of thirteen is thirteen screens. Approving is easy; noticing that two of the four touch the same file is not.
